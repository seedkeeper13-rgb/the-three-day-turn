THE TURN BEGINS BEFORE THE TURN

The first clock marks 31 days of preparation, recognition, and invitation.

During this time, people around the world are invited to discover the Three-Day Turn, read its 66 Understandings, and consider what recognition means in their own lives.

The second clock is already here.

It waits beneath the countdown.

When the preparation period ends, the second clock begins counting upward from zero without requiring anyone to press a button.

Both clocks follow one recorded starting moment, shared across the world. Closing a browser or refreshing the page does not reset that timeline.

The clock is designed for uninterrupted continuation. Its publicly recorded start time allows people to verify its progress. No website can guarantee immunity from technical failures or later modifications.

The future activation mechanism for the 72-hour Three-Day Turn is separate and remains under development.

Participation is always voluntary, peaceful, and nonviolent.

No payment, membership, or allegiance is required.
